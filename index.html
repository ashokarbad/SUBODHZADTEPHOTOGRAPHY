Meet Subodh

Subodh taught himself photography after his family helped him buy his first camera. More than ten years on, he is one of the best-known wedding photographers in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, shooting both traditional and candid coverage.

He is also a musician who has played dhol-tasha since he was a child, so he knows a celebration from the inside: when to step in close, and when to stay out of the way.

The new SUBODH ZADTE PHOTOGRAPHY studio is now open in Tisgaon. Drop in to see albums, talk through your plans, or book a studio shoot.
