Morning

Haldi हळद

Turmeric, laughter and a lot of mess. We stay close and keep shooting, because the best expressions happen when nobody is posing.

Afternoon

Mehendi मेहंदी

Slow hours and fine detail. Hands, patterns, songs, and the friend who keeps feeding the bride while her mehendi dries.

Late afternoon

Getting ready तयारी

Paithani, nath, the last pin in the hair. Quiet portraits before the noise begins, while the light is still soft.

Evening

The part everyone remembers. Traditional coverage for the family album, candid for everything in between, and nobody standing in the priest's way.

Behind the camera

Subodh taught himself photography and has spent more than ten years photographing weddings across Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. He has also played dhol-tasha since he was a child, so he knows a celebration from the inside: when to step in close, and when to stay out of the way.
